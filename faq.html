Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common CampusFind questions.

How do I report a lost item?

Sign in, choose Report Lost, complete the required details and submit.

Is a photo required?

No. Photos are optional, but a clear image can help identify an item.

How do I claim an item?

Open a found listing, choose Claim, and provide details that help verify ownership.

Where do I collect a verified item?

The demo uses the College Admin Office as the fixed collection point.

Will this demo send real emails?

No. This browser-only demo shows local activity messages. Real email requires a backend and email service.

Will my demo data appear on another laptop?

No. Demo data is saved only in this browser. A shared database is needed for campus-wide access.
