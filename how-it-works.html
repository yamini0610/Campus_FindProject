A CLEAR PROCESS

From lost to found, step by step.

Use these steps to report an item, check for a match, and arrange safe collection.

01

Report

Sign in and add item details, location, date and an optional photo.

02

Search

Browse lost and found reports and compare the available details.

03

Claim

If an item may be yours, send a claim with identifying information.

04

Verify & collect

Follow the admin's verification instructions and collect from the College Admin Office.
