OUR PURPOSE

A campus that looks out for one another.

CampusFind is a student-focused lost-and-found platform designed to make reporting and returning belongings more organised.

What we aim to do

  1. Bring lost and found information together in one place.
  2. Make it easy to submit accurate item reports.
  3. Help students search for possible matches.
  4. Support responsible communication between students.
  5. Encourage ownership checks before collection.
  6. Keep report progress clear with simple status updates.
